While scanning, the extension uses background tabs to open Amazon and Walmart pages. Please do not close them. Net profit = Walmart price − referral fee − WFS fulfillment fee − inbound cost − Amazon price. It does not include WFS storage, returns, taxes or Amazon shipping, and prices can vary by ZIP code. Very large gaps are often caused by a mismatched product (different pack size, model or condition), so always open both links and compare before buying.
